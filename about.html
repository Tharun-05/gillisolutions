Our story

Gilli Solutions was founded in 2013 to manufacture complete kitchen ventilation systems that give commercial kitchens the most performance for their money.

We design, fabricate, install and commission every part of the system — exhaust hood, filters, ducting, blower and silencers — and have completed 250+ installations across India. Our aim is simple: a safe, healthy and comfortable working environment in every kitchen we fit.

  1. 2013

    Founded in Bengaluru to build better kitchen ventilation.

  2. 2016

    Expanded operations and earned recognition for quality.

  3. 2023

    Ten years in, with an expanded product line.
