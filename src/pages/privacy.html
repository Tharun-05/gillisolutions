Privacy notice

Short and plain: what we collect and why.

What we collect

When you send an enquiry or use the chat's “Send to the team” button, we receive what you type — usually your name, phone number, email, site location and project details — plus the page you sent it from.

How we use it

Only to reply to your enquiry and to prepare quotes and service visits. We don't sell your details or use them for unrelated marketing.

Services we use

Forms are delivered to our inbox by Web3Forms. Fonts load from Google Fonts, images from ImageKit, and the map on the contact page from Google Maps; these services may log your IP address as part of serving files. The chat assistant remembers your conversation in your own browser only until you close the tab.
