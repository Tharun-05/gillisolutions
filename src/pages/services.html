Design & site survey

The right system starts with your cooking line, your menu and your building.

We visit the site, measure the cooking line and duct route, and look at what you cook and how much. From that we size the hood, filters, ducting and blower together, so the system pulls properly without wasting power.

Maintenance & cleaning

Grease build-up is the biggest fire risk in a kitchen exhaust.

Regular service keeps suction strong, power bills down and grease out of the ducts. We maintain systems we've installed and existing systems from other suppliers.

Repair & upgrades

Smoke not clearing? Blower too loud? We find out why.

Most problems come down to a few causes: clogged filters, an undersized or worn blower, leaky ducting, or no fresh air for the hood to pull against. We diagnose on site and fix what's needed — from a new belt to a bigger blower or an extended hood.

  • {{icon:check}}Weak suction and smoke spill diagnosis
  • {{icon:check}}Blower replacement and upsizing
  • {{icon:check}}Noise reduction with silencers and re-mounting
  • {{icon:check}}Hood extensions when the kitchen grows

Questions we're often asked

How do you work out what size system I need? {{icon:plus}}

Mainly from the length of your cooking line and how heavy the cooking is — a tandoor or wok range needs far more airflow than a bakery oven. The airflow estimator gives a first figure; we confirm it on site, where the duct route and building height also matter.

Do you work outside Bengaluru? {{icon:plus}}

Yes. We're based in Peenya and have completed {{installations}} installations across India. Tell us your site location when you enquire.

Can you service a system another company installed? {{icon:plus}}

Yes — maintenance, repairs and upgrades on existing systems are a large part of our work.

How often should hood filters be cleaned? {{icon:plus}}

In busy kitchens, baffle filters are usually washed weekly, and more often with heavy frying. Ducts and blowers need periodic professional cleaning; we'll suggest a schedule based on what you cook.
