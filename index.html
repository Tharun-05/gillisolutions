One factory, the whole exhaust line.

We make every part air passes through, so each piece is sized for the next. Follow the air from the stove to the roof.

  1. Hood

    Captures smoke and heat over the cooking line.

  2. Baffle filters

    Strip grease out before it reaches the duct.

  3. Ducting

    Carries the air safely through the building.

  4. Dampers

    Balance the airflow and stop backdraught.

  5. Blower

    Pulls the whole system at the right pressure.

  6. Silencer

    Keeps the noise down outside and in.

And the air coming back in: fresh-air collars, grilles and dampers replace what the hood removes, so the kitchen doesn't go stuffy or pull smoke back.
