Before you get in touch

What should I include for a quote?

Your kitchen type, the length of the cooking line, the main equipment (burners, tandoors, fryers, dosa tawa) and the site location. Photos or a floor plan help a lot — you can send them on WhatsApp.

Do you install as well as manufacture?

Yes. We design, fabricate and install the complete system, and offer maintenance afterwards.

Is there a warranty?

Our products carry a 1-year warranty against manufacturing defects. Exact terms come with your quote.
